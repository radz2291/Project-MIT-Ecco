[10/7/26, 8:51:15 PM] Ahmed MIT: It is an ultrasonic acoustic tool.. [tool in oil and gas means a device that goes downhole for certain function]

Logging means data acquisition using sensors to provide oil companies with information about earth layers, oil, well condition, etc

Logging is made using wireline, which is lowering a cable into the well with tools at its end.. the cable has conductors for powering the tool and retrieve data real time. 

LWD, or Logging while drilling is performing data acquisition while drilling the well this means no extra time lowering a cable into the well after been drilled
. LWD saves lots of rig time as is done at same time while drilling

LWD tools send data real time to surface using mud telemetry, mud pulses, but this is only maximum ~10 bits per seconds, very slow rate, can't sens waveforms.. heavey data are stor3d in memory and downloaded  for processing when tools are retrieved at surface after drilling the well.. it is called Memory LWD
[10/7/26, 8:51:40 PM] Ahmed MIT: Our tool, ECCO, is in the category of Memory While Drilling
[10/7/26, 8:55:22 PM] Ahmed MIT: It has ultra sonic sensors [3 separated by 120 degrees around the tool external surface]
Each can work independently to provide full image of the well.. 
Since this is a "while drilling" tool, then it is automatically rotates and moves with the drilling string as client drills the well

This means sensors rotate around tool axis with pipe rotation [always clockwise looking from surface to well bottom]
Also while drilling, the drill strings moves down as they drill deeper

Since rotation speed is much higher than progressing /penetrating the wells, the sensor scans the well walls [wellbore] each revolution

In cased hole
<< explanation>> [section previously drilled and protected with casing them cemented the casing into earth ..]

After a section is drilled, say with 12.25" bit drilled to 3500meter, a casing, which is a steel pipe, typically of 9-5/8" OD, is lowered into the 12.25" section until its maximum depth, here is 3500m in this example, then a liqhid cement is pumped inside the casing, then pushed with rupper plug to the bottom with water or mud, forcing the cememt to U-Tube through opening at the bottom of the casing to fill the space between the casing and the well bore [formation].. now loquid cement is made its way to fill in the gap between the casing pipe and the earth [well wall]. After a specific time, [many hoirs] the cement hardnes and become solid. The cement volume pumped requires peior knowledge of hole volume [geometry] of the 12.25" section.
Now the 12.25" section is cased and any measurement inside it is called cased hole logging.
The well is typically further drilled butbwith smaller bit to pass through the casing, typically 8.5" bit making 8.5" open hole section, say until reach theboil reservoir at 3900m.
A subsequent casung if 7" OD os lowered and cemented similarly.

In open hole, ECCO measures the hole geometry and layers as explained above
