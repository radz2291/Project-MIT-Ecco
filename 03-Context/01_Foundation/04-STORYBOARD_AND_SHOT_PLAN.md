## Sequence intentions and checks

### S01 — Enter the unseen world

Establish place and then remove visibility. The descent serves the technical story rather than becoming an unrelated geological fly-through. Surface, drill string and tool location must make sense. The transition can use cuts or a planned continuous move; the shot plan proposes two generation units to avoid forcing one model to solve the entire transition.

### S02 — First ping

Preserve the client's intended progression: darkness, a ping/brief light, repeated sensing, then an image becomes readable as scanning occurs. The four-second sample is available as an experiment, not a final approved mechanism. Clarify the sample feedback before reusing its exact lighting or pulse design.

Show effect origin, outward travel/contact and persistent revealed outcome. Explain return/measurement in this sequence or its continuation; do not allow a beautiful one-way wave to stand in for the entire pulse-echo process. A distant curved background supports parallax while remaining distinct from the local borehole.

### S03 — Open hole

Geometry first: show why a borehole is not always a perfect cylinder and how the measured profile is useful. Then show formation/amplitude information. Avoid a claim that decorative strata are precise measured lithology. Washout, asymmetry, units and any data-derived overlay need approval.

### S04 — Cased hole

Make the casing/cement/formation relationship understandable. Distinguish cement support from casing wear; they are different technical explanations. Do not show this ENP3 tool inside an incompatible casing diameter. Review the response-to-condition visualization before finishing.

### S05 — Hydraulic enhancement

Reveal the real body design and the three stages in the correct order. Flow paths should explain cuttings transport and wake control. Choose an approved operating mode; do not imply that circulation and rotation are jointly necessary. Retain illustrative flow without invented CFD readings.

### S06 — Engineering output

Keep the main treatment stylized, as preferred in the earlier storyboard. An actual log can appear briefly if permission and source identity are resolved. Do not turn this section into a lengthy software demonstration or imply that an ENP1 log is ENP3 evidence. Preserve Memory While Drilling acquisition and processing context.

### S07 — Product hero / closing

Resolve the film with faithful hardware and restrained MIT identity. Product naming, logo asset, tagline, contact information and final words must be approved. Add exact text/branding during editing/compositing rather than relying on generated typography.
