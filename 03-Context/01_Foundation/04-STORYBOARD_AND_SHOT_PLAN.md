## Shot status and handoff

The older proposed **eleven executable-shot split** was generated before V2's full set of scene corrections. **Do not implement it as the current shot list.** Its historical details remain in Git history. Work shot by shot from the relevant V2 panel sequence, with the following process:

1. Verify the current **V2 section** and its client comments; use the [Master Feedback Register C01–C09](../02_Storyboard/V2/ECCO_Section_04_Cased_Hole_Padlet_v2/MASTER_CLIENT_FEEDBACK_REGISTER.md).
2. Check [Engineering Baseline](02-ENGINEERING_BASELINE.md) and [Open Decisions](06-DECISIONS_AND_READINESS.md); mark uncertainties and release only unblocked study work.
3. Use [MIT Production Identity](03-MIT_PRODUCTION_IDENTITY.md) for client visual character and [Rolladice Directorial Identity](../03_Directorial_Identity/README.md) for camera/composition taste.
4. Produce the **shot-specific** design under [Scene Direction Contract](../03_Directorial_Identity/07-SCENE_DIRECTION_CONTRACT_v0.2.md). That contract owns camera timing/poses, SVG planning, lighting choices and executor handoff, **not this narrative document**.
5. Review a Blender playblast for visual continuity and factual accuracy before final AI finishing.

## Narrative continuity that matters

- **S02 → S03:** S02 teaches round-trip acoustic travel time first. S03 builds on it, then separately introduces acoustic **amplitude**. Do not repeat the full waveform introduction or depict amplitude as geometry-derived.
- **S03 → S04:** Drilling context and casing geometry must be physically compatible. In V2 the BHA returns **upward into existing casing**; do not animate casing appearing around it.
- **S05:** MIT's actual FEA video/images are pending; do not present decorative flow as validated FEA.
- **S06:** Opening a cylinder is a **mapping metaphor**, not a real cut into rock. Memory data are not live telemetry. The example field report is ENP1, not ENP3; permission/public exposure unresolved.
- **S07:** A product hero may use intentional stationary presentation; do not convert that into an operational claim. Signoff needs approved corporate assets.

Detailed product dimensions, rotation mode and source provenance live in the **Engineering Baseline** only. The original source register and pending approval records live in **Decisions & Readiness**. This page contains **narrative allocation**, not duplicate technical specifications.
