# Rolladice Scene Director — experimental v0.2

This skill is a **task workflow**, not a competing taste guide or a client-approved scene catalogue.

## Input route

1. Start with [context authority map](../../README.md) and the specific project's scene/engineering purpose.
2. For MIT/ECCO: read [Storyboard V2](../../02_Storyboard/README.md), [Engineering Baseline](../../01_Foundation/02-ENGINEERING_BASELINE.md), [MIT Production Identity](../../01_Foundation/03-MIT_PRODUCTION_IDENTITY.md) and only relevant open issues in [Decision Register](../../01_Foundation/06-DECISIONS_AND_READINESS.md).
3. For creative decisions: consult [Creative Personality](../../03_Directorial_Identity/01-ROLLADICE_CREATIVE_PERSONALITY_v0.1.md) and [Taste Evidence](../../03_Directorial_Identity/02-TASTE_EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md). Use the [Visual Casebook](../../03_Directorial_Identity/04-VISUAL_DECISION_CASEBOOK_v0.2.md), [Camera Language](../../03_Directorial_Identity/05-CAMERA_AND_MOTION_LANGUAGE_v0.2.md), and [Look Direction](../../03_Directorial_Identity/06-LOOK_LIGHTING_AND_MATERIAL_DIRECTION_v0.2.md) as relevant, not all by default.

## Steps

- Establish source-grounded technical/narrative objective and explicitly mark uncertainty.
- Select a strong compositional premise and meaningful key views.
- Develop camera arrivals and narratively active travel, or intentionally choose cuts/stillness.
- Choose lighting/material/depth treatment to support the actual view and object.
- Use SVG/previs to expose ambiguous geometry before expensive scene work.
- Critique major artistic decisions, including whether the frame speaks and the camera's travel has purpose.
- Deliver **one** [Scene Direction Contract](../../03_Directorial_Identity/07-SCENE_DIRECTION_CONTRACT_v0.2.md) with stage and handoff, not an additional parallel shot specification.
- Build/inspect a playblast and obtain real feedback before updating any preference as accepted.

## Boundaries

**Typography/motion-graphics aesthetic taste is deferred.** Preserve required client labels as unresolved placeholders. Do not assert camera poses or physics without inspecting actual geometry. Do not copy referenced studios' distinctive work.

For finishing an already animated Blender scene, use [blender-animation-reference](../blender-animation-reference/SKILL.md)—a separate skill and step.
