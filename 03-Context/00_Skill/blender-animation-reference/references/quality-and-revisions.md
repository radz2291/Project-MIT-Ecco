# QA and revision

Require frame files, encode/decode success, decoded count/FPS/resolution, exact duration/mapping, source hash preservation and motion checks. Inspect logs/manifests, not Blender's exit code alone. Enable evaluated geometry checks for meaningful deformation. Clearly state the checks' coverage and remaining limitations.

Brightness/variance/identical-frame metrics only flag potential problems. A non-black but featureless frame can be a failed reference; an authored empty/dark/held shot may be valid. Image differences do not prove motion direction. Never automatically repair an intentional hold, occlusion, camera cut or silhouette loss.

Inspect first/last, phase sheets, transitions and consecutive frames around difficult events; preview video where available. Check identification, direction, parallax, scale/proximity, attachment continuity, stable fixed machinery, readable nesting, and accurate reveal timing. Find opaque surfaces hiding a crossing and foreground faces erasing endpoint cues. Check grey separation and absence of unwanted texture, particles, haze, reflections, grids, wireframes, selection/gizmos, annotations or shading flicker. Frustum inclusion does not establish visible landmarks. Labeled review sheets are not clean generator inputs.

First improve admissible shading, grey separation, clean export and timing. Changes to opacity, reveals, camera, geometry or endpoint require session authorization and an adaptation log. If permission is missing, prepare the concrete issue/change rather than ask a vague question. Preserve original source. Do not solve a hovering-above-seabed failure by shortening the animation so it actually stops high. Try matching boundary stills before altering an exact endpoint.

When generated output is available, compare corresponding times and identify the first divergence. Classify wrong path/direction/zoom substitute, speed/easing/hold, endpoint/proximity, identity/proportions/attachment, missed transition, invented subject action, or appearance conflict. Revise the smallest relevant factor; keep before/after evidence and change one meaningful variable where practical. Consider segmentation for complex failures without asserting a universal maximum clip length.

Do not claim generation success from reference QA. Do not spend credits/upload/submit without authorization. Stop at the user's attempt budget or when no actionable evidence supports another revision. Distinguish hypotheses from confirmed results.

Handoff requires video, matching clean boundary frames, necessary event frames, scene explanation, timed prompt, input-role instructions, source/time/adaptation manifest, technical QA and honest visual-review status. Multi-segment work needs a join plan. State unsupported representations or unverified generator features; do not disguise missing renders as completion.
