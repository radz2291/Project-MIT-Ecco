# Agent Handoff / Acceptance Guide — Section 06

**What the images may define:** visual mood, approximate framing, subject prominence, depth cues, cinematic light and on-screen story structure.  
**What the images may not define:** product CAD, recording mode, actual field numbers, physical wave propagation, precise hole-size thresholds, sample count, azimuth clocking, FEA/CFD or 3D reconstruction algorithms.

## Before Blender production

1. Build a shot-accurate 3D data representation with an explicit shared azimuth/depth mapping for S06-A and S06-B.
2. Identify the camera pose at the end of A and opening of B; keep the underlying borehole geometry and wall features identical.
3. Choose a deterministic test dataset for staged construction. Verify feature correspondence between cylinder and flat map (matching angular and depth coordinates).
4. Show geometry and amplitude as separate measured properties; independently approved colormaps.
5. Obtain or request MIT approval for the genuine Mi-VU report page, or an official redacted/approved replacement. The provided ENP1 sample must never be relabeled ENP3.
6. Render keyframes for approval before undertaking final texture and finishing.

## Checkpoints

- S06-A: At exit, can a viewer identify a continuous borehole shape and the distinction between tool and reconstruction?
- S06-B: Does a highlighted feature keep its measured angular/depth address before and after the virtual opening? Are nominal vs enlarged vs tight colors coherent?
- S06-C: Can we prove the on-screen output's provenance and authorization? Does the shot remain readable for a three-second hold?

## Common failure modes to avoid

- Water/mud streamlines becoming an ultrasonic dataset by direct morph.
- Fake blue laser beams replacing interpreted measurement points.
- A physical pipe splitting open instead of a virtual mapped wall.
- A decorative 2D texture unrelated to the same 3D geometry.
- Presenting ENP1 real field data or generated numerical charts as ENP3 real field data.
- Sharing operator-confidential screenshot externally without authorization.
