**Time:** 1:10–1:14  
**Shot:** S06-A  
**Image:** `S06_A_The_Invisible_Takes_Shape.png`  
**Status:** Cinematic direction; not final camera keyframes or technically approved render.

## Cinematic purpose

Reveal that ECCO’s collected measurements become a continuous 3D description of the true borehole wall.

## Camera composition

Long-axis, three-quarter close-up of the actual tool, gradually dollying backward; the measurement surface gains priority as the camera widens.

## What happens

Flow VFX from Section 5 fades. Spatially indexed wall samples appear, join through depth, and form a continuous irregular surface. Nominal white, blue enlargement and rare red tight zones remain interpretations, not literal colored rock.

## Camera movement

Slow dolly out + gentle orbit; no sudden top-down technical view.

## Audience experience

*Invisible downhole measurements become an understandable geometric object.*

## End composition and transition

The completed 3D borehole occupies the composition and is ready to open along an axial seam.

## Engineering / image-reference caution

Actual data are retrieved after the run; do not show live 3D transmission or clone the fictional tool geometry from the image.
