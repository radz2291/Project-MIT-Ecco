**Status:** Cinematic direction; not final camera keyframes or technically approved render.

## Cinematic purpose

Reveal the correspondence between measurements on a curved 360° borehole surface and the equivalent 2D azimuth-vs-depth representation.

## Camera composition

Start high-oblique to read the cylinder, then become front-facing as the unwrapped map settles. Leave horizontal space for the transformation.

## What happens

The 3D wall holds, a thin longitudinal seam appears, and the 360° measurement surface unrolls to a flat geometry map. A separate yellow/brown amplitude map appears alongside; no geometry-to-amplitude conversion.

## Camera movement

Restrained orbit then stabilize while the virtual surface opens.

## Audience experience

*A complex cylinder becomes a readable printable map without changing what was measured.*

## End composition and transition

Flat blue/white/red geometry map alongside the distinct warm amplitude map.

## Engineering / image-reference caution

Do not treat an irregular borehole as a physically stretched steel shell; preserve mapped data coordinates rather than inventing textures.
