## Read this first

Section 2 owns the pulse/echo phenomenon. No animated HUDs, logos or engineering claims are required in the opening.

## Filmmaking and engineering guardrails

- Do NOT interpret the graphic rig-to-earth cross-section as literal visible steel running outside the borehole through opaque strata.
- Do NOT place the camera inside the hollow drill pipe and have it cross through solid steel to the annulus unmotivated.
- Offshore surface, sea/seabed, sunlight, and exact rock layers are unconfirmed production design—not claimed field reality.
- ECCO is part of the BHA; do not mistake it for the drill bit.
- The natural sun cannot reach the deep sensor region. The last dim highlights are selective film lighting.
- The first ultrasonic pulse must occur only after Section 1 ends (Section 2 starts at 0:10).
- Shot C must match S02-A's near-wall close three-quarter perspective and maintain product orientation/scale.
