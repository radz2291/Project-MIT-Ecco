### S04-B — What Lies Behind Steel (0:44–0:49)

**Camera composition:** An intimate oblique side view inside deviated steel casing, with ECCO's imaging sensor and a selectively translucent window into the annulus outside casing.

**What happens:** ECCO emits an ultrasonic pulse; the steel casing responds with ringing. Where cement supports the casing, the acoustic response decays relatively quickly; where a fluid-like or poorly bonded region sits behind it, ringing persists longer. A brief comparison graphic supports the contrast as the backing material gradually becomes visible as an explanatory overlay.

**What the audience should understand:** Differences in the returned ultrasonic response can reveal information about support behind opaque steel.

**End/transition:** One anomalous backing location remains subtly highlighted. Camera begins moving along the casing to investigate its extent.
