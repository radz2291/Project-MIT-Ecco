# Padlet — Section 2 Cinematic Shot Cards

**Section introduction:** Section 2 follows ECCO into a world without direct visibility. One pulse briefly reveals the hidden wall. The returning echo becomes a measurable arrival-time signal. As the drill string rotates, many individual measurements accumulate into an irregular 360° outline. The section introduces ECCO's pulse–echo sensing principle before Section 3 reveals the true hole geometry.

## S02-A — First Contact | 0:10–0:13
**Image:** `S02_A_First_Contact.png`

**Camera composition:** A close, three-quarter view along ECCO's metallic body, framed diagonally with the rough borehole wall close beside it. The environment is almost entirely dark.

**What happens:** One ultrasonic pulse leaves an outward-facing sensor, strikes the rock and briefly illuminates a small area. The echo returns to the same sensor. Two more brief pings reveal separate spots, then fade.

**Camera movement:** Very subtle forward push; no large orbit.

**Audience experience:** Something invisible has just been detected.

**End composition:** The tool is still partially in darkness; a few small areas have briefly been revealed, but the full wall remains hidden.

## S02-B — Sound Becomes Measurement | 0:13–0:17
**Image:** `S02_B_Sound_Becomes_Measurement.png`

**Camera composition:** Slightly tighter view of the sensor and rock, with dark negative space reserved for the waveform. Preserve the sensor ↔ wall spatial relationship.

**What happens:** Another pulse travels out and its echo returns. A delicate waveform appears: transmitted event, reflected signal and the highlighted echo arrival-time interval. Two or three more arrivals are shown before the waveform recedes into persistent wall measurement points.

**Camera movement:** Gentle controlled lateral adjustment as the entire tool rotates.

**Audience experience:** ECCO isn't illuminating the wall; it's measuring how far away it is.

**End composition:** Waveform fades, leaving spatially fixed wall data points.

## S02-C — The Hidden Circumference | 0:17–0:22
**Image:** `S02_C_The_Hidden_Circumference.png`

**Camera composition:** Begin in the three-quarter view, then slowly pull back and rise to an oblique along-axis position. Keep the ENP3 recognizable; use limited near-wall transparency to see around it.

**What happens:** The whole drill string rotates, carrying three sensors on one plane. Repeated pulse–echo measurements become points around the borehole, then irregular connected arcs, and finally the first complete measured 360° outline.

**Camera movement:** Deliberate continuous pullback and shallow arc, not an abrupt top-down cut.

**Audience experience:** Tiny measurements have become a picture of the unseen circumference.

**End composition:** A single irregular measured ring around ECCO that leads directly into Section 3's reference-vs-reality comparison.
