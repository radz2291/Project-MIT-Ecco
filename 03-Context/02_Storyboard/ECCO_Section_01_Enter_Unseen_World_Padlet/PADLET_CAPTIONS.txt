SECTION INTRODUCTION
This opening sequence establishes the physical context of ECCO. We begin at the rig, travel down through the drill string, pass the seabed and earth layers, and finally arrive deep underground where direct human visibility is no longer possible. The purpose of this section is to frame the problem that ECCO solves: it operates in a hidden environment that cannot simply be observed by eye.
