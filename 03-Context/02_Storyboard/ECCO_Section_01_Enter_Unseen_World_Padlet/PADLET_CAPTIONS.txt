SECTION INTRODUCTION
The film opens in the visible world at the drilling rig, then follows the drill string down through water, seabed and rock layers into the dark, inaccessible subsurface where ECCO operates. This sequence establishes the real operational context and the core problem: the well cannot be inspected directly, which is why ECCO’s ultrasonic sensing capability is needed.
