Panel 3 — Borehole geometry through depth (revised)

As ECCO collects measurements at successive depths, the data can be reconstructed into a continuous 3D representation of the actual borehole wall. White indicates nominal drill-bit size; progressively darker blue shows increasing enlargement, while red marks locally undersized or tight regions.

In addition to ordinary enlargement, certain drilling conditions can produce repeating spiral or corkscrew-shaped irregularities along the wall. This illustration shows an example of such wall deformation, with cross-sections demonstrating how the enlarged region changes its position around the circumference as depth changes.

The spiral is part of the measured wall shape—not a rotating ribbon inside the hole. The visual is conceptual and should not be presented as a verified spiral feature from the supplied Mi-VU recording.
