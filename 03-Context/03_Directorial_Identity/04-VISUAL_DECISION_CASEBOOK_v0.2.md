# Rolladice Visual Decision Casebook — v0.2

**Created:** 14 October 2026  
**Status:** Candidate comparisons to evaluate, NOT evidence that the director approved the illustrated solutions.  
**Purpose:** Turn personal taste into context-sensitive decisions that an AI director can use and later critique.

## Reading this casebook

- **Observed** = director's actual feedback recorded in [Taste Evidence Register](02-TASTE_EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md), or a documented ECCO prototype observation in [Production Workflow](../01_Foundation/05-PRODUCTION_WORKFLOW.md).
- **Proposed preference** = our operational interpretation to **test**, not a newly confirmed personal rule.
- **Authority:** MIT technical sources win over any visual preference; do not invent tool geometry, sensor physics or measured values.
- These A/B descriptions are **constructed candidate treatments**, not actual reviewed renders, screenshots, studio work or a proof that the director preferred the mockup.
- Evaluate works at the particular shot or timestamp where possible. Avoid treating reference artists as preset packs.

## Case V01 — A hero product image should communicate without an effect

**Use case:** introductory or closing product presence shot (e.g. ECCO S07-01).  
**Evidence:** E002 (FutureDeluxe: spatial invention, unique framing, each still speaks); E003 (Tendril: premium simplicity).

| Candidate A — common but weaker for this intention | Candidate B — direction to test |
|---|---|
| Generic centered full-object turntable; uniformly exposed; motion must create the interest | Authored relationship between product, camera, foreground depth and deliberate negative space; meaningful view even when frozen |
| No planned showcase besides the full-object view | Opening silhouette → informative component arrival → deliberate final hero view |
| Textures and effects are tasked with making the product impressive | Strong silhouette, proportion and carefully chosen material highlight communicate premium engineering |

**Why B may win:** Image has a point of view. Space gives the product scale without distorting CAD. Camera arrivals become meaningful, not just mechanically smooth.

**When A can win:** An instructional specification view, catalogue comparison or strict orthographic examination may demand symmetry, complete visibility and neutral light.

**Agent's evidence to provide:** annotated first/key/final composition sketches and a one-sentence reason for each camera arrival.

**Director validation:** Has **not** selected Candidate B for any rendered S07 iteration yet.

## Case V02 — Camera travel is part of the narrative

**Use case:** a close mechanical inspection requiring multiple details.  
**Evidence:** E004 (Tendril camera flows, each stop is a showcase, transitions carry a sub-narrative through timing/acceleration/rhythm).

| Candidate A — transit-only | Candidate B — phrased discovery |
|---|---|
| Straight interpolation between arbitrary positions with a constant apparent speed | Plan arrival frames first; design travel to change what is revealed, occluded or understood |
| Camera stops immediately when it reaches a coordinate | Deliberate braking/settling into an authored composition |
| Camera is aimed at a generic object origin throughout | Camera target follows a feature or relation as attention shifts without unintended snaps |

**Why B may win:** The viewer senses intention even between stops. Changing parallax and view create an intermediate visual statement.

**When A can win:** If the shot must communicate a steady calibrated sweep, survey or comparison, constant movement is appropriate.

**Agent's evidence to provide:** plan-view path SVG; key composition thumbnails; a timeline with arrival/transition/settle windows; a rendered preview of the whole move.

**Director validation:** Movement behavior is directly preferred; the exact curves and poses are not yet reviewed.

## Case V03 — A reveal should leave information behind

**Use case:** unseen physical environment becomes legible, such as the ECCO first sensing event.  
**Evidence:** Existing ECCO four-second prototype observations: orthogonal rings read like an atomic symbol; cage-like linework and floating patches confused the wall; a disappearing wall lost the discovery. See [Production Workflow](../01_Foundation/05-PRODUCTION_WORKFLOW.md).

| Candidate A — surface decoration | Candidate B — cause-and-effect reveal |
|---|---|
| Rings/particles appear around the tool with no clear source or contact | Identifiable sensor/interaction origin → apparent propagation/interaction → persistent readable spatial outcome |
| A bright decorative effect conceals shape | Limited visual effect supports tool/wall relationship and time ordering |
| Revealed wall disappears at the end | The informative state remains long enough to understand the discovery |

**Why B may win:** It explains a transition from unknown to information, rather than simply suggesting 'advanced technology.'

**Critical restriction:** This describes **communication logic**, not a validated acoustic field. Client feedback about the light/ping remains unresolved. Do not assert precise physics or finalize this scene's effect before technical review.

**When A can win:** Deliberate abstract branding, a non-factual interstitial or an effect designed solely to express emotion—provided it is not mistaken for a measured phenomenon.

**Agent's evidence to provide:** state diagram with origin/contact/outcome, first/event/final views, persistence check.

## Case V04 — Premium finish through selective lighting (hypothesis)

**Use case:** credible machined-surface hero.  
**Evidence:** E003 explicitly praises premium simplicity; *specific lighting setups are NOT user-confirmed*.

| Candidate A — indiscriminate illumination | Candidate B — deliberate light sculpting to compare |
|---|---|
| Every surface evenly exposed, little silhouette differentiation | Selectively reveal useful faces/edges with motivated key and reflection shaping |
| Numerous highlights competing for attention | Fewer, purposeful highlights at the framing's most important surfaces |
| Brightness treated as quality | Shape definition, material behavior, separation and controlled shadow treated as quality |

**Why B may win:** Supports technical detail and monumental presence without forcing effects.

**When A can win:** Dimensional review, product inspection, defect detection or technical drawings may require broad, even illumination.

**Agent's evidence to provide:** same-camera A/B frames with consistent model and exposure intent; note the features that must remain readable.

**Director validation:** Unconfirmed taste; compare real stills before promoting this to a firm preference.

## Case V05 — Space can feel 'breathing' without being empty

**Use case:** product hero versus an explanatory close-up.  
**Evidence:** E002 strongly values effortless, unstrained images; our interpretation needs empirical comparison.

**Weaker candidate:** Arbitrary empty area or excessive forced asymmetry presented as 'premium.'  
**Candidate to test:** Space and subject relationships create an obvious reading order. A dense close-up may still breathe when surface shapes and attention hierarchy are controlled.

**Diagnosis:** Can the viewer identify the intended hero, second look and point of curiosity in a static frame? Remove or reposition competing elements before changing the whole style.

**When it fails:** If negative space pushes a critical mechanical feature outside the frame or impairs the engineering explanation.

## Case V06 — Separate temporary camera simplification from operational physics

**Use case:** a stationary isolated first ping versus actual downhole scanning.  
**Evidence:** [Engineering Baseline](../01_Foundation/02-ENGINEERING_BASELINE.md) records that isolated stationary staging does not replace rotation/translation when explaining drilling/scan behavior.

**Candidate A (incorrect as a scanning explanation):** Tool remains stationary while the film claims circumferential scanning solely through that staging.  
**Candidate B:** Use a static illustration **only for the isolated first-ping concept**, then show or explain genuine rotation/translation and accumulation in the appropriate scanning shot.

**Why B wins:** Visual elegance must not mislead about the operating mode. This is a **technical correctness constraint**, not a personal taste judgment.
