## Visual decision sequence

1. **What should be visible?** Identify required mechanical surfaces, feature relationships, silhouette and intended information hierarchy.
2. **What should remain concealed?** Choose where darkness/softness enhances discovery, without concealing essential engineering.
3. **What makes the subject tangible?** Shape reflections and highlights so surface curvature and manufactured detail read.
4. **Where does depth come from?** Stage product, local interaction volume and background distinctly; add atmosphere only if it makes space legible.
5. **What is the emotional job?** The lighting should support mystery, intimacy, technological confidence or technical clarity, not simply signal 'cinematic'.
6. **How does the frame breathe?** Assess negative space, hotspots and visual competition from the actual camera views.
7. **What happens across motion?** Verify changing reflections, silhouettes and occlusion at entry, showcase beats, transitions and final arrival.

## Candidate treatments (test; do not silently adopt)

| Treatment | When it may work | Failure to watch | Alternative |
|---|---|---|---|
| Selective narrow highlights and restrained environment | Product silhouette / tactile surface drama | Important geometry becomes unreadable | Broad soft sources / bright reveal |
| Neutral bright soft-field lighting | Mechanism comparison / instructional clarity | Flat, un-authored product presence | Controlled directional gradients |
| Rim/reflection sculpting | Polished cylindrical form, contours | Plastic/glossy exaggeration or incorrect finish | Broader reflection with verified material |
| Subtle depth haze / layered background | Journey into subsurface environment | Fake scale, visual clutter, implies fog physically present | Clean parallax geometry |
| Motivated event light | Illustrative discovery tied to a specific action | Unverified physical sensing light, false physics | Restrained non-literal overlay after validation |

## Working environment: an invisible underground scene

Treat 'invisible' as **a narrative information state**, not an instruction to make a black frame.

**Staging proposal:** isolated readable tool silhouette → limited local interaction readability → a meaningful reveal of surrounding geometry. Keep local wall distinct from more distant parallax elements. If a light/effect depicts ultrasound, it must be labeled an illustration until technical review. The client's concern regarding the earlier first-ping light remains unresolved.

**Camera-light coordination:** place the crucial showable face along the trajectory's key arrivals; maintain shape-defining reflections during travel; verify that the hero does not vanish into an unstructured background. Different lighting setups may be appropriate for each shot.

## Material and render review

- Preserve correct machined silhouette, edges, seams, fittings and scale.
- Use surface texture and controlled roughness variation only to the extent justified by physical reference and art direction. 'Premium' does not mean mirror-polished.
- Keep reflections coherent across camera movement, AI boundary stills and generated motion.
- Separate product appearance from decorative environment treatments in Blender and compositing.
- Inspect raw neutral playblast, candidate look stills, first/event/last frames, and a full moving preview.
- Prefer recoverable masks/passes for precise product geometry and technical effects.
- If AI changes the product shape or spatial state, correct source conditioning/compositing or fall back to conventional rendering; do not accept drift as taste.

## Lightweight comparison protocol

1. Lock one camera/view and one product geometry version.
2. Render two distinct lighting/material hypotheses, changing a documented variable.
3. Compare silhouette, key technical visibility, subject/background separation, tactile plausibility and effortlessness.
4. Record **which option the director selected and why**. Until that happens, neither is an established favorite.
5. Repeat evaluation at moving camera arrivals; a good still does not guarantee stable moving highlights.
