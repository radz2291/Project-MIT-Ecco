# ECCO S07-01 — Proposed 8-second Product Hero Direction Study

**Status:** Conceptual art-direction exercise, not approved shot/camera path or produced playblast.  
**Grounding:** [ECCO shot plan](../../01_Foundation/04-STORYBOARD_AND_SHOT_PLAN.md), [engineering baseline](../../01_Foundation/02-ENGINEERING_BASELINE.md).  
**Taste:** E002 (FutureDeluxe), E003–E004 (Tendril).

## Why this shot?

Present the real tool as a piece of meaningful engineering. The viewer should understand its presence and recognize that this is an engineered object, not an invented sci-fi prop. The shot closes the narrative with confidence and visual relief.

## One creative idea

**Give a faithful engineering object monumental presence through perspective, depth and a short camera journey with purposeful arrivals.**

- **Entry:** An authored silhouette leaves room for the frame to breathe. No forced centered full-product view.
- **First arrival:** Navigate around the actual model to a verified interesting detail; let the surface and construction speak, not a decorative light show.
- **Travel:** A curved or offset move changes perceived scale/parallax with smooth target redirection; the transit is itself part of the discovery, not blank interpolation.
- **Final arrival:** Reveal the product identity and an elegant stable framing for approved end-card information, if required.
- **Exit:** Preserve the meaningful final view instead of dissolving into ambiguous geometry.

## Camera score — deliberately not final numeric animation

| Beat | Illustrative normalized range | Purpose | Verification required |
|---|---|---|---|
| Entry | 0–0.20 | Orient to the silhouette and scale | Model bounds and composition |
| Discovery transit | 0.20–0.48 | Navigate toward an identifiable detail | Avoid clipping / occluded feature |
| Detail arrival | 0.48–0.62 | Let the verified mechanical feature register | Actual CAD feature selection |
| Spatial release | 0.62–0.83 | Redirect gaze and reveal coherent overall form | Camera/target velocity and screen-space behavior |
| Closing arrival | 0.83–1.00 | Make the final frame clear and dignified | Final shot, approved identity and end-card constraints |

These fractional ranges are **design hypotheses** only. No claimed timing curve, lens, approval or real Blender keyframes. Do not force a second showcase if the observed duration feels crowded.

## Camera and geometry

- Try independent camera-path and look-target controls, subject to source Blender project.
- Sketch path in [camera-plan.svg](camera-plan.svg). It is a schematic, **not** a metric plan or actual product silhouette.
- Build first and final composition thumbnails from the **registered product asset**; the SVG does not depict ECCO dimensions or sensor positions.
- Stationary tool for a standalone hero is a staging option, not an operational claim about drilling.
- If the object is shown moving, declare product motion separately from camera motion.

## Lighting / rendering hypotheses

- Start with credible silhouette-defining light and plausible manufactured surface reflections.
- Use a restrained environment to separate form; check shadows and changing reflections at each showcased view.
- Compare a softer brighter treatment against a darker sculptural treatment *from identical cameras*; neither is confirmed Rolladice preference.
- MIT actual material, official brand assets and final wording remain awaiting confirmation.
- Do not apply a generic neon/HUD look simply to suggest advanced technology.

## Review gates and expected assets

**D0 now:** conceptual shot logic and explicit unresolved facts.  
**D1 to achieve:** director selects key stills/camera premise after seeing product-based sketches.  
**D2 to achieve:** inspect actual Blender/CAD; record camera poses, lens, frame/time values, velocity/easing, lighting rig and asset path.  
**D3 to achieve:** inspect a full playblast with first/detail/final frames; MIT verifies any technical claims or features shown.  
**D4 only afterward:** use boundary appearance anchors and AI finishing if suitable.

**Output expectation for execution:** actual first/key/final stills, camera-plan-derived paths with verified transforms, source Blender file, grey playblast and review report. None exist from this study alone.

## Camera critique prompts

Does the first frame already speak? Is the detail worth visiting? Does the travel reveal, not merely relocate? Does the stopping rhythm feel like a sentence with emphasis and release? Does the final composition breathe? Could a simple cut be better?
