# Rolladice Directorial Identity — Context Index

**Version:** 0.2 · 14 October 2026 (v0.1 foundation retained)  
**Status:** Internal working taste knowledge; explicitly provisional and expandable  
**Owner:** Rolladice creative direction  
**Initial application:** MIT Technologies / ECCO, **not** an official MIT brand specification

## Why this belongs here

This folder captures **creative judgment**, not merely shot templates or software instructions. The purpose is to let a human or AI director make distinctive, context-aware choices about spatial composition, camera choreography, lighting, materials, motion graphics, text, editing and restraint.

It is reusable across future Rolladice projects. A client's identity, engineering facts and shot requirements take precedence where applicable.

## Authority and change policy

1. **Client-verified facts, approved geometry and safety/technical requirements** cannot be overruled by aesthetic preferences.
2. **Client branding and contractual deliverables** constrain the application of Rolladice's style.
3. **Rolladice taste knowledge** guides creative choices inside those bounds.
4. **Shot-specific decisions** must be recorded and can deliberately override non-binding stylistic defaults when the reason is explicit.
5. User-confirmed likes/dislikes must remain traceable to direct evidence. Treat inferred techniques, aesthetic theories and unsampled disciplines as hypotheses, not mandates.
6. Never claim an artist's approval, copy their exact creative signature, or present inspiration as a commissioned collaboration.

## Future evolution

Append new reference observations to the evidence register first, compare with existing preferences, then revise the personality and interpretation guide when supported. Keep v0.1 as a baseline. A provisional scene-director skill now exists for experimentation; treat it as **untested** until exercised on actual shot construction and corrected by the director.
