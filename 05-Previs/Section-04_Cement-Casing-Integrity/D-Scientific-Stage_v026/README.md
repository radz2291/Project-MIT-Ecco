## Source and continuity

The approved Section 3 v036 scene and its final review image were inspected. Reused qualities are cool blue-grey staging, fine white/cyan contours, restrained light and the source view transform/look. Section 3's final scene does contain a HUD ground context; this new D treatment deliberately reduces that context under the owner's newly agreed isolated-stage direction. It is continuity of visual language rather than a claim that both environments are identical. The Section 3 yellow/brown amplitude imagery describes different information and is not imported into wear.

Camera matrix, lens and shift are taken directly from D-Physical-Wear_v017, corresponding to the owner's preferred wear view family. The latest v022 steel, fixed computed data and tool placement are retained at frame 400. Soil, cement, channel and fluid context meshes are hidden for this focused presentation. Nothing physically opens, dissolves or flows.

## Tool display and safeguards

All visible hardware receives local presentation-only copies. A uses low-opacity shaded surfaces. B draws actual sharp/boundary CAD edges as curves, rather than a dense triangle grid. C combines faint surfaces and weaker feature lines. These lines identify hardware; they are not acoustic measurements. Source meshes and their dimensions remain unchanged. The existing nominal/current measurement curves and ComputedWearDepth_m values remain unchanged, with maximum authored loss approximately 3.998 mm. The original dashed and worn cyan profiles are not made farther apart.

Each proposal has an editable local scene in Scenes/S04_D_A_v026.blend, S04_D_B_v026.blend or S04_D_C_v026.blend. Exact camera, provenance, geometry checks and render timings are in Records/proof.json. All final stills use 1280×720 EEVEE preview settings; no video was rendered. An initial expensive shadowed-light attempt exceeded the cap and was stopped. Shadows were disabled for the final inexpensive proof lights; final timing is recorded rather than inferred from setup time.

Previous scenes and movies are preserved. Native/media stay local; scripts, records and selected stills are the synchronized handoff. Physical mechanism, final camera transition, temporal recording readability and human acceptance are not approved by this still pass.
