## Taste application and self-critique

**E002 / FutureDeluxe:** A, B and C use different depth and scale relationships. A looks along the tool; B presents a large sculpted surface and its passage; C spreads the working body across the image. Space gives the source object presence without altering dimensions.

**E003 / Tendril:** Existing steel, amber fluid and matte cuttings carry the image. No new decorative effects, labels, lights or materials were added.

**E004 / Tendril:** Travel has a specific task: expose a pad flank, transfer attention to the exiting fluid, then reconnect the regions. Each major arrival has recognition time. These are interpretations of recorded taste, not copies of a reference film.

**Stronger than v018 in the inspected stills:** the sequence no longer repeats approximately the same long diagonal at three scales. The lower view has stronger lengthwise perspective, B opens a different face during its approach, and C resolves into a broad working-body composition with a deliberate held ending. The camera is quieter during the main fragment interaction.

**Remaining questions:** A's accelerator meaning is still qualitative, and C's pressure-recovery intention is not something an image can validate. Both depend on the preserved illustrative mechanism and unresolved functional face mapping. The cut into B and the camera roll during the arc need owner judgment at normal speed. Cuttings become smaller during the final reveal. Existing low-sample grain and occasional wisp/metal alignment are visible; these are reported separately rather than changed during a camera-only test.

**Rejected camera test:** the higher, more end-on final view exposed the finite fluid domain as an amber end disc. The retained C framing avoids that boundary through composition; no mesh or material was changed to hide it.
