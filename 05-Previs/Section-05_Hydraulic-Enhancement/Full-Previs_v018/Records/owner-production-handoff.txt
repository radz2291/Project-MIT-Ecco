## 3. Remaining refinements

There are only two non-blocking visual concerns from my review.

### A. Cuttings material

Some fragments in v017 appear slightly too luminous or golden.

Keep their improved visibility, scale and motion, but make the cuttings look more like natural rock.

Preferred appearance:

- Irregular solid shapes.
- Matte mineral materials.
- Subtle warm-grey or earth tones.
- Reflections appropriate to the environment.
- No self-illumination or spark-like glow.

Do not reduce visibility so much that cuttings become difficult to recognize again.

### B. Environmental context

The close-up in v017 does not fully establish the annular space or surrounding borehole wall.

**Do not solve this by overloading the existing close-up.**

The complete Section 5 sequence now gives us the opportunity to establish that context in Part A, before entering the detailed interaction in Part B.

The v017 close-up should remain primarily about the interaction between flow and engineered pad geometry.

## 4. Approved 18-second story architecture

Our established narrative is:

**Flow accelerates → twisted geometry redirects and agitates it → the integrated hydraulic system manages downstream flow.**

The three functional regions operate simultaneously. We introduce them sequentially only because the camera follows the upward flow.

### S05-A — The Flow Accelerates

**Local time: 0:00–0:05 (5 seconds)**

Purpose: Establish the hydraulic environment and introduce the first functional region.

**Composition**

Begin with a medium three-quarter view showing:

- ENP3 within a recognizable open borehole.
- The annular space between the tool and formation.
- Upward-moving drilling fluid.
- Suspended rock cuttings.
- The accelerator region.

Use our approved stylized scientific environment.

The surroundings should be simplified, clean and readable, not an empty black space or an overly realistic muddy environment.

**Motion**

Start by making the overall upward circulation recognizable.

As attention moves toward the accelerator geometry, show a local change in the flow paths and their motion.

The audience should recognize that the body's geometry influences the moving fluid.

The camera may gently track with the flow, settling into a meaningful view of the accelerator.

**Important:** Don't exaggerate velocity changes into a quantitative claim. Flow trajectories and apparent acceleration remain illustrative.

**Audience takeaway:**

"The shape of the tool influences the movement of the returning drilling fluid."

### S05-B — Engineered Agitation

**Local time: 0:05–0:12 (7 seconds)**

Purpose: Showcase the interaction between the twisted-pad geometry and flowing mud.

This is our main hydraulic discovery.

**Use v017 as the primary visual foundation.**

**Composition**

Move to the intimate three-quarter close-up of the actual raised/twisted pad region.

Preserve the successful appearance, fluid behaviour and amber trajectories.

Make sure the incoming and outgoing fluid regions remain readable.

**Motion**

The tool continues rotating subtly while the annular fluid moves upward.

The motion should demonstrate:

1. Fluid approaching the pad region.
2. Fluid following and being redirected around the changing pad geometry.
3. Local agitation and curved trajectories.
4. Suspended cuttings travelling through the fluid.
5. Flow continuing toward the next hydraulic region.

The flow must remain independent of the steel's rigid rotation while responding plausibly to the changing geometry.

A small, motivated camera arc is permitted, but the camera should not compete with the interaction.

**Timing**

The seven seconds provide more room than the original short proof.

Use the additional time for context, an observable interaction and a meaningful exit—not by arbitrarily slowing the v017 animation.

**Audience takeaway:**

"The sculpted pads have a functional purpose: they redirect the flow and help keep rock cuttings moving."

### S05-C — Diffuser & Integrated Hydraulic System

**Local time: 0:12–0:18 (6 seconds)**

Purpose: Complete the hydraulic explanation and reveal the three regions as one connected system.

**Composition**

Follow the upward-moving fluid toward the diffuser region.

Show the downstream geometry and how the illustrative flow continues beyond it.

Then gradually widen the composition to reveal more of the entire ENP3 hydraulic body.

**Motion**

Begin with a readable view of the downstream fluid interaction.

The diffuser is designed to contribute to pressure recovery and management of the low-pressure wake.

Represent that intention through a controlled transition in the illustrative flow pattern. Do not invent a validated pressure distribution or claim the wake has been scientifically eliminated.

As the camera pulls back, keep fluid moving through all three hydraulic regions simultaneously.

The final composition should communicate that the accelerator, twisted pads and diffuser form one integrated physical design.

**Audience takeaway:**

"ENP3 combines three engineered hydraulic regions to influence fluid flow and support cuttings transport."

The final wider product composition should provide a clean editorial handoff toward Section 6.

## 6. Engineering and operating-mode requirements

Our selected visualization now uses **combined circulation and rotation**, superseding the earlier circulation-only visual setup.

Preserve:

- Upward annular mud return.
- One connected, rotating ENP3 hardware assembly.
- Correct sourced rotation direction.
- No arbitrary axial tool translation.
- Actual source product dimensions.
- Physically open fluid passages.
- Rock cuttings transported with the moving fluid.
- Consistent fluid behaviour across the three regions.

Do not rigidly attach the entire fluid field to the tool rotation.

Ensure moving tracers, volume elements and cuttings do not visibly pass through solid hardware.

The exact geometry-based trajectories remain illustrative.

**Technical verification still required:**

Confirm the actual accelerator pads, twisted-pad sets and diffuser positions using the available CAD, client records and production geometry.

The source register identifies some stage boundaries and pin/box mapping as unresolved. Do not silently invent certified locations.

If the mapping is not independently established, retain the explicitly illustrative interpretation and identify the limitation for the owner.

Do not rescale the 2.400 m source hardware to match the 2.25 m nominal brochure length.

Do not fabricate CFD, FEA, pressure, velocity, turbulence, transport-efficiency or pack-off performance results.
