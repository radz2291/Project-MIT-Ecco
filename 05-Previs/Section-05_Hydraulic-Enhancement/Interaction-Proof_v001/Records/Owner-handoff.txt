### 3. Approved creative decisions

#### Stage 1 — Narrative Intent: APPROVED

Section 5 reveals that ENP3 is more than an ultrasonic measurement instrument.

Its engineered physical body also influences drilling-fluid movement to support the transport of rock cuttings.

The story progresses through three discoveries:

- **A — Acceleration:** Body geometry influences local annular-flow velocity.
- **B — Engineered agitation:** Twisted pads redirect fluid and affect cuttings movement.
- **C — Integrated system:** The diffuser's intended downstream role completes the three-region hydraulic explanation.

The audience discovers the regions sequentially, but the regions are operating simultaneously.

Narration communicates significance. Visuals reveal the physical relationships.

#### Stage 2 — Truth & Mechanism: APPROVED

**Selected operating scenario: circulation-only.**

- ENP3 remains stationary relative to the surrounding well during the demonstration.
- Mud circulates down the drill string and returns upward through the annulus.
- Rock cuttings are transported with the return flow.
- Three accelerator pads influence local fluid velocity.
- Two twisted-pad sets redirect and agitate the flow.
- The diffuser is designed for pressure recovery and downstream wake management.

These are source-described functions, not permission to invent quantitative results.

Do not imply that the three regions activate sequentially.

Do not imply that rotation or tripping is required for circulation-only operation.

The exact hydraulic regions and native-axis direction must be checked against the actual product assets. If the precise mapping remains unresolved, label the proof illustrative and do not claim verified product-specific flow geometry.

No unsupported CFD/FEA performance, pressure, velocity, efficiency or pack-off-prevention claims.

#### Stage 3 — Visual Representation: APPROVED

**Stylized scientific visualization with authentic physical geometry.**

Use the real ENP3 body as the principal object.

Surround it with a simplified, recognizable, already-sectioned borehole.

Represent the fluid with a deliberately controlled combination of sparse particles, subtle fluid presence and directional tracers.

Cuttings must feel like small pieces of rock being transported through the fluid, not sparks or arbitrary glowing particles.

The physical environment and the invisible-flow visualization should remain visibly distinct.

#### Stage 4 — Art Direction: APPROVED

Visual identity: **Premium Scientific Hydraulic Visualization**.

- Physically believable satin-steel ENP3 body.
- Simplified dark-neutral borehole context.
- Clean, restrained materials and lighting.
- Subtle fluid presence, no opaque mud blocking the mechanism.
- Sparse suspended cuttings.
- Elegant, fine flow trajectories.
- Clear interaction with actual pad surfaces.
- Controlled visual hierarchy and reduced environmental clutter.

Avoid thick glowing ribbons, excessive particles, fantasy lighting, neon tunnels, empty black-space product shots or arbitrary swirling motion.

**Color reconciliation:** The previously approved T43 shared direction specifies amber hydraulic paths, while the recent Section 5 proposal used cyan. Preserve both as documented directions. Prefer a small controlled visual comparison before final palette lock; do not silently overwrite either approval.

#### Stage 5 — Camera & Motion Architecture: APPROVED

Use **three connected compositions**, with motivated editorial transitions where they improve clarity.

The visual journey follows fluid moving upward through the hydraulic regions.

Revised 18-second editorial allocation:

**Shot A — 0–5 seconds: Acceleration**

Medium three-quarter view establishes ENP3, the annular space and the returning fluid.

Camera follows the flow toward the accelerator geometry.

The local movement change must be recognizable.

**Shot B — 5–12 seconds: Twisted-pad interaction**

Closer oblique camera composition.

A small controlled lateral arc may reveal the twisted geometry, provided it does not imply the hardware is rotating.

The flow visibly redirects around the pads, with suspended cuttings remaining in motion.

This is the main hydraulic showcase.

**Shot C — 12–18 seconds: Diffuser and integration**

Observe the downstream region, then pull back to establish all three functional regions as one connected body.

The camera must clearly communicate that the whole hydraulic design contributes to the continuous flow path.

These allocations are editorial planning targets. Exact poses, focal lengths and event timing come from inspected geometry and the actual preview.

The new 18-second section duration supersedes the older 12-second allocation for S05 only. Do not automatically retime S06, S07 or the total film.

### 7. Acceptance criteria

The proof is successful only if:

- The body and twisted pads are recognizable.
- Upward flow direction is immediately understandable.
- Flow movement visibly responds to the actual pad geometry.
- Cuttings appear transported by fluid, not independently animated for decoration.
- Flow tracers are readable without overwhelming the steel body.
- No false hardware rotation or axial motion is introduced.
- Geometry and explanatory effects remain distinguishable.
- Preview rendering stays within the performance budget.
- The result is understandable at normal playback size and speed.

Check the physical meaning and visual communication separately.

Do not treat an internally successful technical test as owner approval.
