## S07-B — Product Hero and Capability Recall
**1:24–1:28 · 4 seconds**

### Why the shot exists

The audience has heard about ECCO ENP3 for the whole film without needing a conventional advertisement. Here we finally give it physical presence. The distinctive material design and sensor/hydraulic body make the product credible and memorable. This shot also quietly recalls the five already-demonstrated capabilities, but does not try to teach them again.

### Camera composition

Use the **actual approved ENP3 geometry** in an intimate 3/4 hero angle: titanium/steel highlights, restrained warm/cool rim lighting, black/charcoal backdrop, shallow atmospheric falloff, no dramatic nonphysical VFX. Begin on the sensor and hydraulically sculpted portion of the body, then pull out enough that the overall product form can be read. Avoid changing the ENP3 dimensions or inventing entirely new openings/blades to improve looks.

### What happens

A glancing specular highlight tracks across the body. The camera executes one deliberate glide/orbit revealing the physical design. Around the second half, five **brief symbolic acknowledgments** may appear around the product: wellbore geometry, formation imaging, cement evaluation, casing integrity, and hydraulic enhancement. These should be restrained motifs or very short callouts, ideally one by one, not five paragraphs or a complicated HUD. If the narrator's closing phrase lands earlier, they may be reduced or omitted in favor of a purer product hero.

### Suggested atomic moments

1. 1:24.0 — handoff from S07-A; tool body partly framed, material details dominant.
2. 1:25.0 — camera tracks over actual pad and sensor geometry; reflection turns the surface sculptural.
3. 1:26.1 — camera widens; full or nearly full tool body is recognizably presented.
4. 1:26.7 — five capability motifs resolve around, beside, or briefly across the tool; no more than one idea is demanding attention at a time.
5. 1:27.7 — motifs fade, leaving ENP3 in a clean hero pose ready for the identity closing.

### Camera behavior

One continuous controlled dolly/orbit, not an arbitrary spinning turntable. Preserve geometry and screen direction. Deliberately reduce movement near the end. Keep the body inside safe title/action areas.

### Audience experience

*This single downhole tool combines all five functions we've just learned.*

### End composition

ENP3 stands as a resolved object, uncluttered by diagrams.

### Accuracy and aesthetics

The product is a Memory While Drilling device with three imaging sensors at 120° on a single plane, integrated mud sensor, and three-stage hydraulic module. The actual ENP3 model—not generative image details—governs sensor location, pad count/position, housing construction, surface dimensions and textures. A hero photograph must not imply all five sensing modes are simultaneous measurements in one physical operating configuration.
