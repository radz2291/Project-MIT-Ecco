## Common event contract

Portable JSON schema: `event-contract_v001.json`. A-SENSING owns event production; A-CASED consumes it, with a declared fixture permitted during parallel construction. No duplicated pulse or global waveform system. A-FLOW owns its continuous paths and does not consume acoustic events.

Coordinates are metres in the common unscaled T44 assembly/world frame. Store emitter local reference plus sampled world pose, emitting and receiving sensor identity, world hit position/normal, wall/library/version, and event times in seconds. Same-sensor send/receive is mandatory. `send_s < contact_s < receive_s`; timing is illustrative, never calibrated acoustic speed. A contact sample remains world-fixed after sampling even if the assembly moves. Event IDs are stable and distinguish fixture from actual native ray intersection. Surface patches are local to the contact with a bounded lifetime. Three arcs each direction stop at contact; no wall penetration. Response is explicitly qualitative short/long decay after contact, no quantified bond/thickness claim.

Fixture and actual event adapters must expose the same fields. T47 must replace the fixture with a real T46 export before final integration is claimed. Native shared assets publish top-level collections, controls, source hashes and sidecar manifests; consumers link relatively. Use versioned successors for revision propagation and preserve cameras, time ranges and animation while relinking both consumers.
