Overview
The ECCO® ENP3 from MIT Technologies veriﬁes open-hole geometry, casing
condition and cement integrity while eliminating additional wireline runs. It
extends the ECCO platform with a non-magnetic body and a proprietary
three-stage hydraulic enhancement module that improves borehole cleaning
and reduces pack-oﬀ risk.
ENP3 is a Memory While Drilling system
based on multi-frequency, multi-sensor
ultrasonic pulse-echo technology. Three
high-frequency imaging sensors, spaced
120° apart on a single sensor plane
located 60 cm above the lower (pin)
shoulder, record borehole geometry in
open hole and map cement behind casing
in cased hole.
A dedicated mud sensor performs in-situ
logging of mud acoustic properties,
providing real-time velocity correction of
every imaging measurement.
The tool records independently of
LWD/MWD in the BHA, in both rotating
and sliding modes, with optimized
dynamic resolution at drilling speeds up
to 180 RPM and selective command
during tripping without rotation.
The hydraulic module — accelerator,
twisted-pad and diﬀuser stages —
conditions the annular ﬂow around the
tool, keeping cuttings in suspension and
eliminating the low-pressure wake zone
where pack-oﬀ typically initiates.

Hydraulic Enhancement Module
The ENP3 proprietary hydraulic module is built as three sequential stages that optimize the annular mud pressure and ﬂow
proﬁle around the tool, improving cuttings transport and reducing pack-oﬀ risk.
STAGE 1
Hydraulic accelerators
Three pads accelerate the annular ﬂow
entering the module, raising local
velocity to lift and transport cuttings.
STAGE 2
Twisted pads
Two sets of twisted pads generate
optimized mud jetting and controlled
turbulence, preventing cuttings beds
from settling.
STAGE 3
Diﬀuser
Recovers ﬂow pressure and eliminates
the low-pressure wake zone behind
the pads — the primary initiation point
for pack-oﬀ.
Independent hole-cleaning action
Each operating condition activates the module on its own — hole cleaning does not depend on rotation and circulation
occurring together.
Operation Hole-cleaning mechanism
Rotation only Twisted pads act as an impeller, imparting tangential and axial momentum to the mud and agitating cuttings
beds on the low side.
Tripping only Axial movement drives mud across the twisted pads, which act as a turbine and educt the surrounding mud
— no rotation or circulation required.
Circulation only Annular ﬂow is accelerated, jetted and diﬀused through the three stages with no string rotation, maintaining
cuttings transport while sliding.
Combined Eﬀects are additive under any combination of rotation, tripping and circulation.
