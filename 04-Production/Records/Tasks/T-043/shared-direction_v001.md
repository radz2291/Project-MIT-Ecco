# T43 — Shared visual direction v001
Date: 18 October 2026
Status: PROPOSED FOR RADZI REVIEW. No component or shot is released by this document.

## Direction
**A hidden world becomes understandable.** Narration leads the story; each image supplies a tangible discovery. Preserve V3's 22 shots and 90 seconds. The visual progression is physical confinement → local acoustic contact → structured information → the instrument and its maker.

Keep the contrast between rough mineral surfaces and manufactured metal. As information becomes the subject, reduce environmental detail rather than add layers of interface decoration. Camera travel changes what we understand; arrivals give the discovery time to register. The quiet confidence of the closing should feel earned by the journey.

These are proposed film-specific choices, not new universal taste rules. FutureDeluxe spatial presence, Tendril purposeful camera travel and the established Spatial Cinematic / Editorial Minimal preferences inform the choices. No external reference work was newly inspected in this pass.

## VD01 — Surface and descent
**Recommendation:** retain the board's offshore opening as an illustrative setting: a restrained rig silhouette, quiet water and warm horizon. Use a motivated match cut on the well axis into the descent, rather than attempt a continuous journey through kilometres of opaque earth in three seconds.

**Appearance / behavior:** the opening has breadth; the descent gains nearby rock parallax and progressively loses available visual information. S01-C arrives at the sensor/wall relationship that S02-A will retain. No acoustic flash before 10 seconds.

**Fixed:** surface setting is not a claimed field location. ECCO remains above the bit in a connected BHA. Camera access is a cinematic cutaway; no passage through solid steel.
**Controls:** rig visibility, water/terrain plate, near-rock layers, cutaway, atmosphere intensity; camera axis matched across cuts.
**Consumers:** SURFACE/BHA/WELL/MUD; S01-A/B/C, adjoining S02-A.
**Cost:** simple water shader or static plate, low-detail rig, layered rock; no ocean simulation or world-scale volumes.
**Radzi decision:** pending v001.

## VD02 — Hardware, rock and fluid
**Recommendation:** satin steel appearance, broad controlled reflections with narrow edge highlights, restrained machining detail. A wet mineral wall gives tactile contrast. Keep the annulus visibly confined; use selective cutaway/visibility to read the actual clearance.

The tool feels physically present before it becomes a product hero. S07 uses the same material identity with a cleaner reflection environment. Do not introduce a different, shinier fictional product at the end.

**Fixed:** T5 plugged v002 mesh and T6 v003 dimensions; no silent 2.400 m-to-2.25 m rescale. Three sensor references remain on one plane. Finish is art direction, not an alloy claim. Fluid is present even when optically simplified. Generic adapters and bit get only the visible detail needed; no unsupported thread close-ups.
**Controls:** approved roughness/lighting overrides, environment layer masks, mud visibility, shot-specific cutaway; geometry and sensor positions remain master-owned.
**Consumers:** ECCO/BHA/WELL/MUD/CASING; all physical shots, S06-A and S07-A/B/C.
**Cost:** surface shading, reflection cards, bounded transparency; parallax before fog. No compulsory volumetric scattering.
**Radzi decision:** pending v001.

## VD03 — Sound, contact and measurement
**Recommendation:** retain cyan outgoing arcs and emerald returning arcs, with three restrained arcs in each direction. At contact, a small curved membrane and fine local patch mark the wall interaction; the patch briefly dims while the acquired point persists. These are illustrative graphics, not emitted visible light.

S02-A offers one clearly readable event plus two abbreviated glimpses. S02-B introduces a small waveform in the available negative space: transmission, first echo arrival, and a quiet interval arrow. Proposed copy: **Echo arrival**; use **Sensor-to-wall distance** only if needed. No formula or invented numbers. S02-C widens to reveal the accumulated irregular circumference.

**Fixed:** same sensor sends/receives, true wall contact, no propagation beyond the wall; whole assembly rotation; world-fixed samples; one-plane payoff before S03. Waveform is sourced synthetic or explicitly schematic; artistic event timing is not ENP3 firing cadence.
**Controls:** event timing, opacity, arc width, local patch density/lifetime and signal visibility; hit positions and event identity remain data-owned.
**Consumers:** ACOUSTICS/DATA/GRAPHICS with ECCO/WELL/MUD; S02-A/B/C and adapted S04-B/D.
**Cost:** instanced curves/mesh arcs and a small surface patch; avoid volumetric light and dense persistent grids.
**Radzi decision:** historical colors/contact preferences retained as a candidate; exact v001 film treatment pending.

## VD04 — Geometry, amplitude and continuity
**Recommendation:** white nominal reference and blue measured enlargement remain registered at identical scale. Present the existing tight-hole case as a brief, clearly separated comparison in S03-A, rather than paint red into an unrelated enlargement dataset. Proposed small cue: **Tight-hole example**. Return to the retained main case before S03-B. If the director/executor cannot make that transition readable within six seconds, bring that specific timing conflict back; do not invent a combined dataset.

S03-B releases attention from ECCO to the wall through depth. Use a few data-derived contour edges and a grazing view to reveal irregularity. Spiral is optional in V3: include it only where the selected case supports it and the case change is legible. Do not spend the shot on another acquisition tutorial.

S03-C changes the displayed quantity with a distinct graphic cue: **Echo strength**. Let geometry coloring recede before independent yellow/brown amplitude becomes dominant. Preserve wall coordinates; the rock does not physically change and blue is not mathematically converted to brown.

**Fixed:** immutable source values, fixed transfer functions, missing returns remain missing, explicit interpolation and synthetic provenance. Do not manufacture contrast or infer rock type/oil. S06-A/B reuse one identified case and seam; actual output may be a different record and needs a clear editorial cut.
**Controls:** case/channel, sample cutoff, approved mapping, surface/contour visibility and camera; no arbitrary value edits.
**Consumers:** DATA/GRAPHICS/OUTPUT; S02-B/C, S03-A/B/C, S06-A/B/C, S07-A.
**Cost:** mesh attributes/textures and sparse contours; avoid layered translucent cylinders.
**Radzi decision:** pending v001, especially separate-case comparison.

## VD05 — Steel, support, channel and wear
**Recommendation:** establish S04 as a later upward pass with a purposeful cut to a wider view of the existing casing shoe. ECCO crosses into steel before the lower bit. Keep that geography clear before moving close.

A limited cutaway reveals tool/fluid → steel → cement → rock. For support, show a compact qualitative short-decay/long-decay comparison. The supported/weakly supported contrast motivates an outer-annulus reveal; do not show a beam photographing cement through steel. The blue channel stays narrow, on one side outside the casing, and does not flow like a river.

For wear, cut back inside and graze along a shallow pre-existing groove. Keep the exterior unchanged; use a registered nominal inner contour so the increased local radius is intelligible.

**Fixed:** no identified fluid chemistry from color alone; no quantified bond strength or direct thickness claim. Wear is detected, not created by the pulse.
**Controls:** layer reveal, qualitative response, channel bounds, wear/reference visibility; retain physical ordering and source geometry.
**Consumers:** CASING/ACOUSTICS/GRAPHICS/WELL/MUD/ECCO/BHA; S04-A/B/C/D.
**Cost:** bounded section masks and localized graphic response; no simulated vibrating steel or stacked full-volume transparency.
**Radzi decision:** pending v001.

## VD06 — Flow as a distinct visual language
**Recommendation:** illustrative circulation-only mode for the first implementation. The body can remain stationary while continuous slender blue streamlines and sparse matte cuttings travel uphole. Sound uses discrete outward-and-return arcs; flow uses continuous axial travel. This makes the distinction readable without changing the board's flow palette.

Use a deliberate cut from casing to open-hole/body detail at 58 seconds. Move along the return flow, arrive at the twisted-pad region, then widen to unite the body. All three hydraulic regions operate together; camera emphasis advances, not stage activation.

**Fixed:** exact stage/end mapping must be inspected against the real source before feature-specific arrows or labels are released. No calibrated velocity, pressure legend, CFD/FEA claim or quantified wake reduction. No fake particle/geometry penetration.
**Controls:** illustrative path/speed, density, cuttings visibility and stage emphasis; circulation-only is a proposed mode, not a restriction on real operating capability.
**Consumers:** FLOW/ECCO/BHA/WELL/MUD; S05-A/B/C.
**Cost:** deterministic instanced paths and bounded particle count; no fluid simulation.
**Radzi decision:** pending v001.

## VD07 — Graphics, evidence and closing
**Recommendation:** calm sans-serif type, composed with the imagery; spatial anchoring where it clarifies distance or location, stable screen-space waveform/text where reading matters. Keep final font choice editable until availability/licensing and moving legibility are checked.

S06 is a later processed-data space. Let the same sampled surface open along a fixed seam to an azimuth-by-depth map. Camera settles during the transformation. Amplitude appears separately. At S06-C, use authorized real output when available; otherwise a clearly labeled **Illustrative data** demonstrator is the proposed fallback, never fictional field evidence. An authorized ENP1 example must retain ENP1 identity.

For S07-B, prioritize the product's shape and a confident arrival. Do not force five new text labels into its four seconds. Optional familiar capability motifs can briefly recall earlier scenes; a clean hero is the recommended fallback if they compete with narration.
For S07-C, retain the supplied mark, with the icon-only treatment as the review candidate consistent with the earlier closing preference. Keep the original full logo available; no redraw or inferred final MIT brand approval.

**Fixed:** no live-log streaming, no ENP1-to-ENP3 relabeling, no unpermitted field pixels, no new performance slogan. Radzi controls narration and final assembly.
**Controls:** editable copy, visibility, anchor, type scale, output crop, logo choice and fade; source content/aspect fixed.
**Consumers:** GRAPHICS/OUTPUT/BRAND; S02-B, S03-A/C, S04-B/C/D, S06-A/B/C, S07-A/B/C.
**Cost:** native text, thin graphic curves and flat output plates; shallow focus can be composited if appropriate.
**Radzi decision:** pending v001; real-output permission remains unresolved.
