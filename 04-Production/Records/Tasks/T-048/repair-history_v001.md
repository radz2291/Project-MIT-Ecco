# T48 bounded repair history

18 October2026. Preserved source masters were never modified. These findings are executor self-checks, not independent verification or human acceptance.

1. During initial construction, self-referential particle-scale/constraint drivers caused a dependency cycle. A separate normalized parameter empty now drives each path constraint and emphasis; current builds have no dependency-cycle warnings. An initial nested-library path remap also misresolved source dependencies; migration now rewrites only direct references and leaves nested relative paths owned by the T44 libraries. No renders used these construction trials.
2. Initial consumer v001/v002 files passed radial-envelope checks but inspection of sampled world trajectories found a0.5m axial registration error: Follow Path already applied its target's world offset, then a particle parent applied it again. Preserved v001/v002 natives and their original evidence are superseded. Repaired v003/v004 consumers remove duplicate parenting and verify both finite radial and longitudinal envelopes. They preserve distinct cameras/timings and material revision propagation. No source hardware/wall enlargement occurred.
3. The first master-granted six-frame benchmark of v004 passed0.646–1.669s, but actual A36/B48 images showed sub-mm octahedra were hard to distinguish. Those natives/images/timings remain under the original paths. Readability successor `Hydraulics_v003` keeps the0.7mm radial cutting envelope and stretches only its authored axial shape to6mm; matte cuttings stay non-emissive. Slender12mm axial amber dashes travel along the continuous guides to make flow direction visible. Their radial envelope is0.45mm. Source geometry, clearance, ten continuous paths and all-region simultaneity are unchanged. Final consumers use v005; no older candidate is relabeled as the final preview.
4. While migrating the successor, fresh-open had correctly pruned an unused linked collection while retaining linked geometry data. Migration now explicitly loads the canonical collection to add linked tracer wrappers. No rendering used that interrupted migration.

Render and source hashes are preserved in their versioned evidence. Future camera/pose/source changes must rerun finite-envelope checks and the strict<5s full-write benchmark. Exact physical stage-face/end mapping remains unresolved and is not disguised by the readability repair.
