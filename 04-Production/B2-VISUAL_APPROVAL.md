# B2 — Paired Blender execution and visual direction

Updated 16 October 2026, following Radzi's T7 experience. **T26 and the Blender mechanism tickets work as a pair. T26 completion is not a prerequisite for B2 execution.** This supersedes the earlier serial direction-package gate and its blanket no-Blender/no-motion wording. Original engineering dependencies, human approval and technical verification remain in force.

## The working loop

1. **Blender establishes a grounded baseline.** The mechanism agent uses actual registered ECCO/environment geometry to show a cheap still or reuse an existing frame. Identify scale, proximity, occlusion, available space, constraints and practical freedoms. Do not wait for an invented final image before inspecting reality.
2. **Radzi discusses that frame with T26.** The direction partner uses project sources, Directorial Identity and the Scene Director skill to explore how the message should read. AI-generated images are optional aids for appearance/VFX, not mandatory outputs or engineering authority.
3. **Radzi selects a bounded change.** Record the frame/version, concrete question, selected treatment, what to preserve and what remains open.
4. **Blender implements and verifies.** Change the real scene within that direction, return a matching still and report material spatial/engineering issues. Technical implementation choices belong to the executor; bounded experiments are allowed when they answer a concrete question cheaply.
5. **T26 critiques with Radzi as needed.** Repeat only where the result exposes a real decision. Do not create another long document for every turn.
6. **Animate approved treatments.** Obtain explicit approval of the relevant visual treatment and authority for the motion scope. Later appearance/engineering changes return only the affected beat to review, not the whole mechanism or all B2.

**No whole-ticket finish-before-start gate:** T7–T11 retain technical dependencies but do not depend on T26 being Done. T26 can remain active across B2 while mechanisms progress independently on approved decisions. Lack of a generated image or completion of another mechanism's visual package is not a blocker. A particular unresolved visual choice may hold only that affected work.

| Owner | Responsibility |

This is a workflow between separate sessions, not permission for agents to contact one another, spawn agents or begin unassigned execution. Radzi can relay the relevant frame and decision. T26 does not mutate an executor's scenes. It may read scene reports, dimensions, constraints, stills and prior decisions to keep suggestions feasible.

## What carries between the pair

Keep the handoff small: **current frame → concrete question → Radzi decision → bounded Blender change**. Use the existing Scene Direction Contract and per-mechanism decision notes instead of competing specifications. Record exact frame/version links and source status; include VFX form, thickness, spatial scale, transparency, falloff, origin/contact/return behaviour when those choices matter. Note which features must match and which are implementation choices.

Keep shared notes/references under `04-Production/Direction/B2/B201/` through `B205/`, with a short coverage index in `Direction/B2/README.md`. Executor scenes/previews remain under their existing mechanism folders; task reports stay in `Records/Tasks/T-<number>/`. T26 is a consolidated support ticket, not a second owner of Blender assets. Organize and sync permitted work under the production closure rules.

Read the [active Directorial Identity index](../03-Context/03_Directorial_Identity/README.md), [Scene Director skill](../03-Context/00_Skill/rolladice-scene-director/SKILL.md) and relevant modules. The mechanical storyboard supplies information beats and client corrections, not a locked cinematic layout or independent engineering proof. Translate its meaning into a model-integrated explanation with purposeful composition and minimal necessary graphics.

Generated imagery can clarify a look but may ignore physical scale, geometry or proximity. The actual scene bounds what can be implemented. Do not distort the engineering or hardware merely to match a generated reference. Separate sourced facts, supported inference and illustrative effects; raise only consequential uncertainty.
