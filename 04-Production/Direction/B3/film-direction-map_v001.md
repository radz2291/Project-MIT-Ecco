# MIT ECCO — The hidden wall becomes readable

## Recommended direction

**Follow the audience's growing ability to read an inaccessible place.** Begin beside a drill string disappearing into darkness. Find the actual tool and one sensor. Let a small wall contact and its returning echo turn that darkness into a distance. Widen to discover that many such readings describe an irregular space. Then reveal the other information carried by the response: acoustic variation, casing support and internal wear. Show the physical body shaping returning mud. Finally step out of the underground setting into recorded information, unwrap it into a readable map, and resolve on the tool that made it possible.

The emotional progression is curiosity → one understandable discovery → a larger spatial revelation → practical engineering evidence → confident product recognition. The audience includes engineers, stakeholders and investors; the newcomer needs a clear result while the expert can recognize the correct relationship. No claim of oil detection, certified well safety, quantified savings or investment return is proposed.

The strongest visual is **the camera letting ECCO leave the centre of attention while the acquired wall remains**. In S02 the sensor owns the frame. In S03 the discovered space becomes the hero. In S06 that space becomes an intelligible document. These are three different narrative jobs; we do not replay the same reconstruction three times.

## Image and edit language

- Compose depth with an identifiable foreground edge, the tool/wall relationship and a quieter background. Darkness conceals the unknown but leaves orientation legible. Monumentality comes from framing and parallax, never dimensional exaggeration.
- Give every move a destination: a sensor face, the nominal-versus-actual gap, a changing shape through depth, a behind-casing relationship, a complete hydraulic body. Hold where the audience must recognize a result. Use a cut when travel would consume explanation time.
- Move from context → intimate mechanism → open spatial result → legible side comparison → tactile body → clean data stage → full-product resolution. Avoid a repeated axial tunnel/orbit composition.
- During engineering beats, keep one dominant variable at a time. Physical rock, illustrative signal, reconstructed geometry and interpreted map must remain distinct. Use short quiet sans-serif copy where it carries essential meaning. Spatial integration leads S02, with a simple camera-facing timing graphic for reading; no fixed font/linework style is claimed approved.
- Candidate look: low-key mineral context at entry; broad enough reflections to identify the sensor and casing; neutral explanatory cutaways; restrained semantic colors; clean final product space. This is a lighting intention, not a material/alloy claim or final finishing specification.
- Sound proposal: a restrained event accent and response cue, with a quieter recognition moment. Neither is an instrument recording. Music/VO are unselected. The current concept needs minimal readable wording, not a technical voiceover to rescue unclear imagery.
